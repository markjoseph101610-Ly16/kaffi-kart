About us

Hello Kaffi is a Hello Kitty-themed café in Meycauayan, Bulacan, serving the best wings and pasta in town since 2020.

From saucy wings, creamy pasta and rice meals to iced coffee, milk teas and unlimited promos, everything is made to share with family and friends. Dine in, pick up, or have it delivered to your door.
