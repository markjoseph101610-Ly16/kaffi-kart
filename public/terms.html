5. Cash on Delivery (COD) and Pay-on-Pick-up Terms

In these Terms, "COD" means both Cash on Delivery and Pay on Pick-up, that is, any online order that you pay for in cash after you place it. The rules in this Section 5 apply equally to both.

Important: COD is limited to PHP 500.00 per order and PHP 500.00 in total per customer per day. Customers who cancel three (3) COD orders within thirty (30) days may only pay by GCash for the next thirty (30) days.

5.1. Maximum COD Amount Per Order

Hello Kaffi accepts COD only for orders with a total amount of Five Hundred Philippine Pesos (PHP 500.00) or less.

5.2. Daily COD Limit

The total of all COD orders you place in one day, whether for delivery or pick-up, may not exceed PHP 500.00. The day starts and ends at 12:00 midnight Philippine time. COD orders that you cancel still count toward this daily limit. Placing several smaller COD orders to go past the limit is not allowed. Once you reach the limit, you must pay for further orders that day by GCash.

5.3. Orders Exceeding the Limits

An order that is above PHP 500.00, or that would take your COD total for the day above PHP 500.00, is not eligible for COD. For such orders, you must select GCash at checkout. The System will not accept the order on COD.

5.4. Cancelled Orders and Temporary COD Pause

If you cancel three (3) COD orders within any period of thirty (30) days, COD (including Pay on Pick-up) will be paused on your account for thirty (30) days, counted from the third cancellation. During the pause you may continue to order, but you must pay by GCash. The System shows a warning when you are one cancelled order away from the pause. Only cancellations made by the Customer are counted.

5.5. Exact Payment

For COD orders, you are encouraged to prepare the exact amount. Riders and the counter may not always have sufficient change.

5.6. Payment Upon Delivery or Pick-up

COD payment must be made in full at the time of delivery or pick-up, before or upon receipt of the order. Failure to pay may result in cancellation of the order and possible restriction of future COD privileges.

5.7. Right to Refuse COD

Hello Kaffi reserves the right to refuse or cancel COD orders at its discretion, including but not limited to suspected fraudulent or prank orders, repeated failed deliveries or missed pick-ups, or delivery locations outside our serviceable area.

5.8. Failed Deliveries and Missed Pick-ups

If you are unavailable to receive and pay for a COD order, or you do not pick up an order you placed, the order may be cancelled. Repeated failed COD deliveries or missed pick-ups may result in the loss of COD eligibility.
